Sheet music converter

Upload a PDF or photo of sheet music, a MuseScore file or a MusicXML file, and get it back as MuseScore, MusicXML, MIDI and PDF, at a practice tempo. Reading a scan can take a few minutes.

Score
Tempo

Practice apps let you speed a piece up but often not slow it down, so the first tempo starts slower. Every original marking stays on the score; a label such as "Practice tempo: 100 BPM (original 120 BPM)" is added above the first one. Tempos are in quarter-note beats.

Output

You still have to set the title, instrument and composer yourself in Violy's form: pick them from Violy's lists, because typed text alone is ignored.

Uses Audiveris (AGPL-3.0) and webmscore (GPL-3.0). Source: ezraulo/pdftomusicxml (AGPL-3.0).
